About GDP Clinic

Physiotherapy support that doesn't stop when you leave the room.

Gilead Digital Physiotherapy Clinic (GDP Clinic) helps people recover from pain and injury with guidance they can actually follow: structured, physiotherapist-designed recovery programs you can start today, and real physiotherapists available on video when you need a professional eye on your progress.
